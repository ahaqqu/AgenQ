DATA COVERAGE — how far back each harness's telemetry reaches, and how finely it can be attributed
◌ reading telemetry histories… the first pass can take a while
Every harness keeps its own telemetry for its own length of time, and records usage at its own resolution — AgenQ can only chart what is still on disk, at the grain the telemetry actually supports. A range that starts before a harness's coverage is marked partial for that harness (its numbers there undercount, they are not wrong); a range finer than a harness's own resolution leaves that harness's usage out of the chart rather than spreading a coarser bucket across it.
